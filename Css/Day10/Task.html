Dr.A.P.J.Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (1931-2015) was an Indian aerospace scientist and statesman who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. Widely known as the "Missile Man of India" for his pivotal role in developing ballistic missile and launch vehicle technology, he also played a key technical and political role in the 1998 Pokhran-II nuclear tests.
